Making stickers…

Getting them into GoodNotes

One sticker

Tap Copy, go to GoodNotes, then tap and hold the page where you want it and choose Paste. It comes in with no background, so you can move it, resize it and turn it.

Keep it for later

In GoodNotes, circle the sticker with the lasso, tap it and choose Add Element. It goes into your Elements, sorted into collections like a sticker book.

A whole set

Tap Save all under My stickers and choose Save Images, which puts them in Photos. Then in GoodNotes, open Elements, make a new collection and import them from Photos. Make a sheet lays them all out on one page instead, to print on sticker paper or to add as a page.

Several stickers from one photo

Tap Pick the thing, box the first part and tap Keep. Peel goes straight back to the whole photo so you can box the next part, and so on. If the cutout already found separate things, like three people standing apart, Keep each piece separately makes one sticker of each in one go.

When the cutout isn't right

Erase and Bring back are brushes; pinch (or use the + and −) to zoom in for the fiddly bits. Magic wand takes out a colour you tap, which is the quickest way to clear a plain background or the paper behind a doodle. Remove piece deletes a whole stray bit in one tap. Under Edge, shrinking by a pixel or two gets rid of a halo of the old background.

Just the cutout

Every sticker comes out as a PNG with a see-through background. Under Style, choose Cutout only for no border and no shadow at all. The checkered button on the preview shows what's see-through.

Something else?

They're ordinary PNG pictures, so they work in Notability, Freeform, Procreate, Messages, Canva and anywhere else that takes a picture.
