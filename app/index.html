My stickers

Stickers you copy, save or keep land here, so you can grab them again later. They stay in this browser.

Peel works entirely in your browser. The cutting out is done by IS-Net (Apache-2.0) running on ONNX Runtime Web (MIT). Part of stuffs.

Getting them into GoodNotes

One sticker

Tap Copy, go to GoodNotes, then tap and hold the page where you want it and choose Paste. It comes in with no background, so you can move it, resize it and turn it.

Keep it for later

In GoodNotes, circle the sticker with the lasso, tap it and choose Add Element. It goes into your Elements, sorted into collections like a sticker book.

A whole set

Tap Save all under My stickers and choose Save Images, which puts them in Photos. Then in GoodNotes, open Elements, make a new collection and import them from Photos.

Something else?

They're ordinary PNG pictures with see-through backgrounds, so they work in Notability, Freeform, Procreate, Messages, Canva and anywhere else that takes a picture.

When the cutout isn't right

Pick the thing lets you draw a box around what you want, which also helps when it's small in the photo. Erase and Bring back are brushes for the rest.
